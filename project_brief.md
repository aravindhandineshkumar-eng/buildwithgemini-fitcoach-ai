# My agent: FitCoach AI

One-liner: A conversational agent that helps fitness enthusiasts plan workouts and select gear with a catalog of workout routines, exercise plans, and athletic equipment.

Tool coverage:
- Memory: User fitness goals, injury history, current fitness level, and owned gear
- Tools: Look up exercise routines, search athletic gear recommendations, log completed workouts
- Catalog/UI: Catalog of workout routines and gear items to render as A2UI cards and tables
- Image gen: Generate custom workout motivation images and gear visualization mockups
- Sandbox: Compute heart-rate training zones (Karvonen method), target paces, and weekly training volume

Recommended for every project: memory, storage, tools, image generation, A2UI
Agent-specific / stretch (pick what fits): Code sandbox for training zone & pace calculations, Cloud Trace for performance monitoring
